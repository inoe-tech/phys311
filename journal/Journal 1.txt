Reflect on your first numerical solve. What surprised you?

I had a little bit of trouble trying to debug my code, and at times that can be
a little frusterating. I remember in part B of the assignment when I was doing
the Angle Sweep part I had a bug that shifted my graph by 5 degrees. In hindsight
it was a very easy problem to solve but in the moment I checked everying but what
was causeing it. In the end all I had to do was move one section of the code to the
start to fix it. I had a few other debugging issues too but overall that one was the
most annoying because of its simplicity.

What surprise me was how long it took to do this assignment. I really didnt think it
would take me around five hours to do. It was also surprising how easy it was to loose
track of time while I did it.
