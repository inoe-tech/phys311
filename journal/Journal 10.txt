What surprised you about designing for a user rather than for yourself? 
What's your final project going to be?

I found myself caring more about how the simulation looked in the web 
browers than how many bugs there were or how well it ran. After all first 
impressions are everything. In my experiance a user (like me) is more 
likely to come back to something that is a work in progress if it is 
somewhat functional and if it looks good.

I personally really like planet sims so I am thinking about doing 
something that is along those lines. I doubt that I will be able to do 
everything that I want to with it but I think this project will be the 
most enjoyable for me to work on.
